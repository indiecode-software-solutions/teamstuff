Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

CapOasis ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web-based intranet services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect includes:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, corporate identification, and phone number that you voluntarily give to us when registering with the intranet.
  • Device Information: Information about your mobile device, such as your device ID, model, manufacturer, operating system version, and general system settings (e.g., vibration/haptic features).
  • Usage Data: Logging information regarding your interactions with our corporate portal, directories, files, attendance reports, and chat systems.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain corporate intranet features (attendance tracking, files management, company forum, and directory tools).
  • Improve performance and enhance user experience (e.g., configuring smooth transitions and tactile haptic feedback).
  • Secure corporate workspaces through encryption, isolating per-company environments and credentials.
  • Generate files upload permissions, directory sync states, and local device interactions.

3. Sharing of Your Information

We do not share your personal data with third-party advertising companies. Your data remains strictly isolated within your company's private tenant workspace. We may disclose information only if required by law or to protect corporate security integrity.

4. Data Security

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your corporate and personal information. All tenant workspace data is encrypted and isolated per company environment.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, please contact your organization's system administrator or reach out to our team.
